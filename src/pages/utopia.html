Utopia

2017

The project uses gamification and children's participation to develop an innovative digital democracy tool. In the interactive game Utopia World, the starting point is "we go further and reach higher when we collaborate". The project's primary target groups are children aged 9-13 (grades 4-6) as well as actors working on democratic method development and technology-based learning for children.
