Consent forms for people with limited previous education

2026

A consent form that has to be read leaves out the people it most needs to reach: respondents who do not read Swedish, or do not fully master it. This project replaced the written form with a series of explanatory videos that set out the rights and practicalities of taking part, so that refugee interviewees with limited previous education can be recruited, and give informed consent, on grounds they actually understand.

The videos were produced in Adobe Illustrator, After Effects and Premiere, and made in six language versions: Swedish, Arabic, Swahili, Dari, Persian (Farsi) and Tigrinya. The work was carried out at Umeå University in 2026 as a user project within InfraVis. Read more about the project at InfraVis

Working this way is a step towards more inclusive and ethical research practice, in line with the FAIR and CARE principles for handling data. The illustrations on this page are by Linnéa Tjernström, who produced the videos.
