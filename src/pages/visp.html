VISP - Visible Speech

2020–

The Visible Speech (VISP) infrastructure provides access to a centralized environment for spoken language research that strives to meet both researchers' need for efficient workflows and the legislator's requirement of secured data management. The infrastructure offers the research community a unified environment for conducting reproducible speech signal processing in an environment that supports national and international collaborations. The platform covers the most comprehensive set of publicly available procedures for speech and voice analysis in the world.

Digital archiving of hosted projects is also made easier by a uniform, documented, and transparent directory structure for all projects in VISP, which also reduces barriers to making data available in accordance with the FAIR principles.

VISP is a collection of tools rather than a single application, most of them running in the browser. TRATT transcribes speech orthographically, and Artic labels, visualises and corrects speech recordings and the signals derived from them. Both run on the user's own computer, and both can be tried without an account.
