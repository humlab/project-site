Staden
An interactive video installation of a city rendered through dance

2014

The documentation of the project Staden was created from the idea that a city consists of parallel, ongoing events. Nomodaco, in collaboration with Humlab and the production company Lampray, presents an interactive video format that can be experienced at any time online. Staden was part of Umeå 2014's web broadcast program, and the ambition was to transfer the core of the performance to a new format that accommodates different conditions and possibilities. Through the internet, an exclusive and time-bound event is transformed into a more general, flexible, and personal experience.

This is the end result of an artistic research project developed in collaboration with the dance company Nomodaco. It is a video based interactive art installation.
