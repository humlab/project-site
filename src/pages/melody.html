Melody

2018–2021

MELODY is a response to the European Union's action plan for CBRN incidents, which underlines the obligation of Member States to provide care to all those affected in the event of a CBRN incident. The purpose of the project is therefore to develop a harmonised curriculum for CBRN training for emergency and healthcare personnel within the EU.

The objective of MELODY is to define, develop and deliver a curriculum for CBRN training, based on self-experienced needs and shortcomings among active emergency and healthcare personnel within the EU. The underlying idea is that a joint effort to prepare emergency personnel for CBRN incidents increases efficiency, cross-border cooperation, and strengthens overall preparedness against CBRN threats across the EU. The project consists of ten partners from nine different countries and is led by Dr. Carlos Rojas Palma, SCK•CEN, Belgium.
