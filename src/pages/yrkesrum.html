Yrkesrum

2018

Recent research has shown that regional economies change gradually over time. In principle, it can be said that regional resources (competences, activities or technologies) that are related (complementary) to already existing regional resources are more likely to develop in a region compared to resources that are further away from current specializations. This is because the region can reuse existing competences in new activities, which among other things facilitates local competence supply.
