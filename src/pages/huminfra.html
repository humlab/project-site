Huminfra

2022–

Huminfra is a Swedish national infrastructure supporting digital and experimental research in the humanities by providing users with a single entry point for finding existing Swedish materials and research tools, as well as developing national method courses. Huminfra is coordinated by Lund University Humanities Lab, and comprises 12 nodes at 11 universities and organisations.

The web-based information platform compiles and links to Swedish digital and e-research data sets, tools, expertise and educational opportunities, and makes these easily identifiable and accessible. Humlab at Umeå University is one of the nodes. Read more about the infrastructure
